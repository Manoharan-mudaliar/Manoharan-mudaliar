### What I'm building

**[ThreatLens](https://www.thethreatlens.com)** is the AI data-security and governance platform that lets enterprises adopt AI without losing control of their data. It sits between people and every AI surface — chat, agents, APIs, the browser, endpoints, Microsoft 365 — and governs each interaction in real time, with evidence.

- **Govern every AI interaction** — DLP, policy, model routing, and a tamper-evident audit trail on every prompt and file.
- **Govern agents, not just chat** — agentic workflows governed per step, so DLP, policy, routing and audit apply on every hop an agent takes.
- **Trace the full AI data lifecycle** — lineage of sensitive data from source, through every AI touchpoint, to where it lands.
- **Surface the shadow AI** — discover unsanctioned AI use across the browser and the endpoint.
- **Automate the SOC** — agentic analysts, GRC, and assurance on one governed core.

> Bring your own models. Keep your data yours. Prove it with evidence.

Heads-down contributing to **ThreatLens Core** — a durable, agentic **security-operations** engine for autonomous threat detection, investigation, and response. One of the products in the broader ThreatLens ecosystem.
